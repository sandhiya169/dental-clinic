Our story

Built slowly, on purpose.

Stackly didn't start as a five-room studio. It started as one chair, one hygienist, and a stubborn idea — that a dental visit could be unhurried, honestly explained, and something you'd actually look forward to.

Dr. Naomi Voss Founder & Lead Clinician

How we got here

Twelve years, one quiet promise.

  1. 2012

    Stackly opens its doors

    One converted bookbinder's studio, one chair, and a promise scrawled on the intake form: no rushed appointments, ever.

  2. 2015

    The studio expands

    A second treatment room and our first dedicated hygiene team, so cleanings never had to compete with same-day emergencies.

  3. 2018

    Molds retired for good

    Digital intraoral scanning replaces the last of our impression trays — faster, and far more comfortable for anxious patients.

  4. 2021

    Same-week care begins

    We commit publicly to seeing every new patient within a week — and have kept that promise in every quarter since.

  5. Today

    14,200 smiles, and counting

    Still the same unhurried pace we started with — just with a few more hands, and a lot more practice.

What we believe

Four ideas we don't compromise on.

I

Unhurried by design

Every appointment slot is built with breathing room in it — for questions, for nerves, for a slower pace than a typical practice allows.

II

Explain before we touch

Every instrument, scan, and step gets described in plain language first — you'll never feel a tool before you understood why it's there.

III

Material honesty

We use the same crowns, composites, and aligners we'd choose for our own families — nothing swapped out to save a margin.

IV

Quiet technology

Digital scanning, guided implants, and 3D planning — all in service of the visit, never the centerpiece of it.
